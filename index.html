-- Blinkit_Customer_Segmentation.sql Engine: MySQL 8.0 
SELECT 
    customer_segment,
    COUNT(customer_id) AS total_customers,
    ROUND(SUM(total_order_amount), 2) AS total_revenue,
    ROUND(AVG(average_order_value), 2) AS avg_order_value
FROM blinkit_customers
WHERE registration_date >= '2025-01-01'
GROUP BY customer_segment
HAVING total_revenue > 1000000
ORDER BY avg_order_value DESC;

Business Objective: Segregate Blinkit customer cohorts by revenue contribution and calculate average basket sizes to isolate the top-performing demographic segment.
